# Stock Market Fundamentals — One-Page Summary

## What is a stock market?
A stock market is a regulated marketplace where investors can buy and sell shares and other securities. A share represents a unit of ownership in a company. Share prices can rise or fall and investors may lose money.

## NSE and BSE
- **NSE:** National Stock Exchange of India.
- **BSE:** BSE Ltd., formerly Bombay Stock Exchange.
- Both provide electronic markets for eligible listed securities and publish market information. A company may be listed on one or both exchanges.

## Nifty and Sensex
- **Nifty 50** tracks 50 major companies listed on NSE.
- **Sensex** tracks 30 major companies listed on BSE.
- An index summarizes the performance of a selected group; it is not the same as the price of one company.

## IPO and SME IPO
- **IPO (Initial Public Offering):** when a company offers shares to the public and seeks listing for the first time.
- **SME IPO:** an IPO by a qualifying small or medium enterprise on a designated SME platform, subject to exchange and regulator rules.

## Market capitalization
Market capitalization = current share price × number of outstanding shares. It is a market-value measure, not the same as revenue, profit, or cash in the bank.

## Common metrics
- **P/E ratio:** share price ÷ earnings per share (EPS). It compares market price with earnings; interpretation depends on growth, risk, sector and accounting.
- **P/B ratio:** market price per share ÷ book value per share. It compares market price with accounting net assets per share.
- **EPS:** profit attributable to ordinary shareholders ÷ weighted-average ordinary shares. Check whether the figure is basic or diluted and whether it is consolidated or standalone.
- **Dividend:** a distribution to eligible shareholders when declared by the company. It is not guaranteed.
- **Bonus issue:** additional shares distributed to eligible shareholders in a stated ratio; the number of shares changes, but this alone does not create extra total value.
- **Stock split:** each existing share is divided into multiple shares, reducing the face value per share proportionately; this alone does not create extra total value.
- **Trading volume:** number of shares traded during a period. Volume alone does not tell whether a company is financially strong.

## Company financial statements
- **Balance Sheet:** assets, liabilities and equity at a particular date. Basic equation: Assets = Liabilities + Equity.
- **Profit & Loss (P&L) / Income Statement:** revenue, expenses and profit over a period.
- **Cash Flow Statement:** cash generated and used in operating, investing and financing activities.

## Basic financial ratios
- **Current ratio** = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities; a short-term liquidity indicator.
- **Debt-to-equity** = Total debt ÷ Shareholders' equity; a leverage indicator. Define the debt measure consistently.
- **Net profit margin** = Net profit ÷ Revenue × 100%.
- **Return on equity (ROE)** = Profit attributable to owners ÷ average shareholders' equity × 100%.
- **Operating cash flow** helps compare reported profit with cash generated from operations.

## Data analyst's checklist
Always record the reporting period, currency, units (rupees, ₹ crore, etc.), consolidated vs standalone basis, source URL, and download date. Compare like with like. Ratios need context and should not be treated as automatic buy/sell signals.
